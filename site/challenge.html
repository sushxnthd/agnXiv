Evidence-neutral protocol

A challenge records a divergence under a declared setup. It does not allege misconduct and does not by itself establish that a paper is universally false.

The public beta uses GitHub for the final authenticated submission and author-response thread.

Challenge lifecycle
  1. 1
    Divergence

    You document a specific mismatched result.

  2. 2
    Inspect

    Evidence, environment, and target attempt remain public and attributable.

  3. 3
    Respond

    Authors or other researchers can identify missing details or corrections.

  4. 4
    Resolve

    Future reruns can resolve the discrepancy or confirm persistent divergence.
