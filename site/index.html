01 / VERIFICATION CONTRACT

From paper to
evidence record.

AgnXiv separates discovery from verification. It can use heuristics to find a claim or workflow, but it cannot issue a positive numeric verdict until the tested claim, source, runtime, locator, and comparison policy are explicitly bound.
