How AgnXiv works

Verification, not vibes.

Discovery can be probabilistic. A final computational verdict must be tied to exact executable evidence.

01

Compile claims

Extract material quantitative claims and map each one to the paper, code, data, figures, and reported value.

02

Reconstruct evidence

Pin the repository commit, infer dependencies, identify the experiment workflow, and bind every declared artifact.

03

Execute in isolation

Run research code inside a hardened, no-network sandbox with immutable host evidence and bounded resources.

04

Issue a record

Compare observed output against an explicit policy and publish the provenance needed to inspect or replay the result.

Built for scientific workflows

One verification layer, three audiences.

AgnXiv is designed to make executable evidence legible to the people who create, review, and depend on computational research.

Researchers

Prove what actually reproduces.

Turn a paper and repository into a claim-by-claim verification record without asking readers to rebuild the experiment themselves.

Reviewers

Audit evidence, not screenshots.

See the exact commit, environment, workflow, observed output, blockers, and abstentions behind every computational verdict.

Labs & publishers

Add a verification layer before trust.

Use a common evidence format for reproducibility checks, robustness tests, and eventually private editorial workflows.
