# Centrality, Leakage-Free Locators, and Claim-Evidence DAGs

Centrality: annotate primary contributions, central empirical claims, supporting claims, and incidental numbers. Evaluate recall@k and NDCG.

Locator: mask reported numeric values until the output file/path/metric/split locator is frozen. Evaluate exact-locator accuracy and abstention.

DAG: annotate claim → paper evidence → output → script/function → model/checkpoint → data → preprocessing → environment. Score node/edge precision-recall plus critical executable-path recall.

Models may assist all three discovery tasks; none of these scores can directly set the final numeric verdict.
